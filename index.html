Information density

Stored information is log₂(states). Density divides it by cell area. A three-state cell therefore represents approximately 1.585 bits in this metric.

density = log₂(states) / area

Layout-normalized energy

Figure 7(b) divides reported search energy by layout factor k. This is a derived comparison metric; it is not the raw energy measurement.

k = area / (node in µm)²

Faithful data, interactive styling

Each view excludes missing axis values and unreported storage modes, following the released scripts. Technology colors are consistent here; the published Figure 6(b) colors points by process node.

A fixed research snapshot

The 41 released records are the survey dataset, not an exhaustive or continuously updated ranking. Devices can differ in operating conditions and evaluation methods. Voltage and match-line width are shown verbatim where recorded in the source workbook. Full array size and measured/simulated status are not recorded in dedicated fields and are left unspecified.
